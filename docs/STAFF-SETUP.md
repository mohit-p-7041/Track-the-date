# Setting up the real staff

Do this once, before the first staff session on Sat 15 Aug. Twenty minutes at the laptop.

Until it is done every entry is stamped `BP TECOMA`, which is the one thing PINs exist to prevent.

---

## What is there now

Two accounts came across from beep, both still on the placeholder PIN `1234`:

| Account | Entries it holds | What it actually is |
|---|---|---|
| `BP TECOMA` | 2,290 | The old app's **shop login**. The reference screenshot shows Team "Tecoma", Members: 1 — everyone shared it |
| `sar ob` | 50 | Looks like one person, under a shorthand |

## The rule that decides what to do with each

`batches.added_by` points at the account row, not at a copy of the name. **Renaming an account
renames every entry it ever made.**

So:

- **A rename is right when it is the same human under a better name.** `sar ob` → `Sarah O'Brien`
  is honest: those 50 entries were hers, and now they say so.
- **A rename is wrong for a shared login.** Renaming `BP TECOMA` to a person would make 2,290
  entries claim that person made them, permanently mixed in with the ones they really do make.
  Nothing would record that those entries came from the old shared account.

`BP TECOMA` gets **taken off the list** instead. The row stays, so its 2,290 entries keep saying
`BP TECOMA` — which is the truth: the old app knew the shop, not the person.

The Settings screen shows the entry count next to every name so this is visible at the moment of
deciding.

---

## The steps

Sign in as `BP TECOMA` / `1234` and go to **Settings**.

**1. Rename `sar ob`** to whoever it is — type over the name, press Rename. Reset the PIN to one
they choose. Their 50 entries follow the new name.

**2. Add everyone else.** Name and a 4-digit PIN each, one row at a time. Around ten people.

Let each person pick their own PIN if they are there. If not, give them anything and they can
change it later — it is not a password, and anyone can reset anyone's.

Names are matched without case, so `Sarah` and `sarah` cannot both exist. Use whatever staff will
recognise on the sign-in screen — first names are fine, and two Sarahs need a surname initial.

**3. Take `BP TECOMA` off the list.** This is the last step, because you are signed in as it.

The moment you do, that session can no longer add anything — the next thing you try to save sends
you to the sign-in screen. That is intended. Sign in as yourself and carry on.

It disappears from the sign-in screen, so nobody can tap it by habit. It moves to a card at the
bottom of Settings with a **Put back** button if this turns out to be wrong.

**4. Sign out every iPad.** Each one holds a cookie that lasts thirty days. An iPad still signed
in as `BP TECOMA` cannot add anything after step 3 — it will land on the sign-in screen — but it
is better to walk round them once than to have somebody meet that mid-scan on Saturday.

## Check it worked

Two people, two entries, two names. Not a screenshot of the settings page.

1. Sign in as person A. Add a date to any barcode.
2. Sign out. Sign in as person B. Add a date to a different barcode.
3. Open both products from **Products**. Each date should read *Added by A* / *Added by B* with
   the right time against it.
4. Open the sign-in screen and confirm `BP TECOMA` is not on it.

If step 3 shows the same name against both, stop — something is wrong with the session and it is
worth finding out before Saturday rather than after.

## Afterwards

- Anyone who leaves gets taken off the list the same way. Their entries stay.
- The list cannot be emptied — the last active account refuses to be taken off, because that
  locks the shop out of its own laptop.
- There is no admin. Anyone can add anyone, rename anyone, and reset anyone's PIN. That is the
  decision (SPEC §2): about ten people, one shop, and a gate would only mean waiting for whoever
  holds the key.
